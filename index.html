The challenge

One girl. Multiple needs. One connected pathway.

Anaemia, nutrition concerns, emotional distress, and pressure to leave school or marry early can overlap. Support works better when services can identify needs safely and coordinate appropriate follow-up.

🩸

Physical health

Help girls understand warning signs and connect with qualified health workers for assessment and care.

🥗

Nutrition

Offer practical, locally relevant nutrition education and pathways to available nutrition services.

💜

Emotional well-being

Make supportive information and trained human help easier to find, without stigma or diagnosis.

📚

Education & safety

Provide a safe way to explore support when a girl faces pressure, unsafe situations, or barriers to schooling.

🤝

Trusted follow-up

Help authorised health teams keep track of referrals and unresolved needs.

📊

Community insights

Use aggregated information to identify where screening or services may not be reaching girls.

Private sample tool

A gentle health check-in.

Answer only what you feel comfortable sharing. This demo uses your answers only in this browser session to show possible next steps. It does not send or save them to a server.

🔐 Your privacy: Do not enter your real name, address, phone number, or identifying details. This prototype is not monitored by a health professional.

In a real deployment, screening questions and escalation pathways must be reviewed by qualified adolescent-health and safeguarding professionals.

1. What would you like support with?

Choose any that apply. “Prefer not to answer” is always okay.

2. Have you been able to speak with a trusted adult or health worker?
3. What would be helpful next?
This is not a diagnosis or a validated clinical screening tool. If you feel in immediate danger or have severe symptoms, seek help from a trusted adult and local emergency or health services.
